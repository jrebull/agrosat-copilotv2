\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}

AgroSatCopilot is organised as a perceiver/reasoner system. The
perception stack (Section~\ref{sec:method-perception}) converts
Sentinel-2 time series into per-parcel observations; the conversational
layer (Section~\ref{sec:method-bemyeyes}) reasons over those
observations and communicates with the user.

\subsection{Perception stack}
\label{sec:method-perception}

\paragraph{Inputs and features.}
Each parcel is represented by (i)~the raw multi-temporal Sentinel-2
patch (10 bands), (ii)~spectral indices (NDVI, NDWI, EVI, MSAVI2,
MCARI, CCCI, NDRE) and temporal features (FFT harmonics, phenological
calendar descriptors), and (iii)~the 64-dimensional AlphaEarth
annual embedding~\cite{brown2025alphaearth}. Frozen
DINOv3-satellite features~\cite{oquab2023dinov3} provide a
self-supervised complement.

\paragraph{Dense segmentation models.}
We train and evaluate temporal and spatial architectures on PASTIS-R.
The temporal family comprises U-TAE~\cite{garnot2021utae} and
TSViT~\cite{tarasiou2023tsvit}, the latter extended with a phenological
branch (TSViT-pheno) following~\cite{wen2025phenology}. The spatial
baselines are U-Net~\cite{ronneberger2015unet},
DeepLabv3+~\cite{chen2018deeplabv3plus}, and
SegFormer-B0~\cite{xie2021segformer} run on a three-band RGB input.
AnySat~\cite{astruc2024anysat} is evaluated as the foundation-model
backbone. No Swin-UNETR model was trained.

\paragraph{Tabular classifier.}
An XGBoost classifier~\cite{chen2016xgboost} over the AlphaEarth
embedding summarises the annual spectral signature in 64 dimensions and
acts as a decorrelated, low-latency member of the ensemble.

\paragraph{Contrastive vision-language branch.}
FarSLIP~\cite{li2025farslip} (ViT-B/16) plays two roles: it contributes
class probabilities to the ensemble meta-model, and it generates a
natural-language phenological description of each parcel that is
injected into the grounding block delivered to the reasoner. The
phenological FarSLIP component, including the diagnosis and fix of its
contrastive-alignment bug and its faithful-vs.-AlphaEarth comparison,
is detailed in its dedicated section (Section~\ref{sec:farslip_method},
contributed by US-072).

\paragraph{Heterogeneous ensembles.}
The base probabilities feed two heterogeneous combiners trained on
\emph{out-of-fold} predictions to avoid leakage: a balanced logistic
regression stacking meta-model, and an Optuna-weighted blending. The
stacking meta-model acts as a per-class arbiter, weighting each member
differently by crop type rather than averaging.

\subsection{Conversational layer: the Be My Eyes pattern}
\label{sec:method-bemyeyes}

\paragraph{Perceiver/reasoner separation.}
The perception models inspect a parcel and emit a plain-text observation
(crop type, phenology, vigour, confidence) without sending tensors or
logits to the LLM. A \emph{frozen} reasoner consumes that text, invokes
geospatial tools as needed, and drafts a natural-language response.
Every figure in the response originates from an auditable
\texttt{tool\_result} in the event stream, following the \emph{Be My
Eyes} pattern~\cite{huang2025bemyeyes}.

\paragraph{Frozen dual reasoner and data sovereignty.}
The same agent supports two interchangeable, frozen backends:
Gemini~3.5~Flash in the cloud (the deployed default, chosen for its
cost/latency profile)~\cite{google2025gemini25}, or
Qwen3-30B-A3B~\cite{yang2025qwen3} on-premises via vLLM
(GPTQ-Int4, single GPU) for clients that require their data never to
leave their perimeter. Backend selection is handled by a factory
without touching business logic. The reasoner is never fine-tuned as a
classifier; Gemma-4 LoRA fine-tuning is out of scope for this work
(the model's fused 3D MoE experts preclude the planned QLoRA route).

\paragraph{Geospatial toolset.}
A closed set of ten Pydantic-validated tools is exposed: five
synchronous (list parcels, time series, area statistics, classify a new
parcel, explain prediction) and five deferred, executed asynchronously
via a background worker (scene search, map tiles, save area, compare
models, retrieve RAG context).

\paragraph{Spatial-RAG.}
To reduce hallucinations, a spatial pre-filter (\texttt{ST\_DWithin} on
PostGIS) is combined with semantic search (cosine similarity over
64-dimensional AlphaEarth embeddings in pgvector). Retrieved documents
are phenological descriptions of real neighbouring parcels. The
multi-region label-space harmonisation (HCAT macro space) that enables
cross-region transfer is developed in its dedicated section
(Section~\ref{sec:multiregion}, contributed by US-073).
